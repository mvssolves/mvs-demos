The house

We make so little of it that consistency was never on the table.

Two stills, both small, both older than anyone working them. We fill perhaps forty casks a year and bottle them one at a time, when each is ready rather than when the calendar says so.

That means no house style to defend and nothing blended away. A cask that turns out quiet gets bottled quiet. It also means that when you find one you love, there is a real chance you will never taste it again.

What we will not do

Colour it. Chill it. Blend it to a target.

Caramel colouring makes every bottle look like the last one. Chill-filtration stops the haze and takes the texture with it. Blending to a target is how a distillery stops being a place and becomes a brand.
