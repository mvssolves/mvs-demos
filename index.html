MVS Solves — demo builds

Five sites, five systems.
Nothing shared between them.

Each one is built from its own tokens, its own type pairing and its own motion language. That is the point — a house style applied to every trade is how agency work starts looking like a template.

01 Grange & Son

Architectural stone yard. Hard chalk-and-cobalt split, ultra-condensed display, monospace spec sheets, machined easing. Photography desaturated at source so one colour carries the page.

 Open → 02 Coldharbour

Single-cask distillery. Paper and ink, a didone set wide, letter-spacing used as the layout, and almost no motion at all. The only colour is the amber in the photographs.

 Open → 03 Wren Street

Private women's health practice. Paper and iodine violet, a capsule crop used on every framed thing, abstract micrographs instead of stock medical photography. Booking is the whole page.

 Open → 04 Blacksmith Row

Barbell gym. Ink ground, one sulphur accent that only ever marks a number or a button, solid-and-outline display type over peak-action photography. Timetable is a real table.

 Open → 05 Ridley & Vaux

Commercial disputes set. Warm shell, oxblood, a text serif doing everything and mono for the record. No photographs of handshakes — credibility comes from the index and the numbers.
